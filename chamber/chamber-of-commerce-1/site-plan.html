Ruwa Chamber Connect: Website Plan

This living document outlines the direction, structure, and style decisions for my chamber home page build.

Ruwa Chamber of Commerce Homepage

1. Site Name

Site Name: Ruwa Chamber Connect

Optional domain: ruwachamberconnect.org

This name was selected because it clearly identifies the city focus (Ruwa), the business audience (Chamber), and the purpose of the site as a networking and information hub (Connect).

2. Site Purpose

The website provides a central place for local businesses and community members to find chamber news, membership benefits, upcoming events, weather highlights, and business spotlights. It also supports member growth by providing a clear path to apply for chamber membership.

3. Scenarios

  • How do I join the chamber, and what membership level fits my business?
  • Which local businesses are currently featured or recommended by the chamber?
  • Where can I find upcoming chamber events and announcements this month?

4. Color Scheme

The following colors define the visual identity for this planning document and the project direction.

Deep Teal - #0F4C5C

Used for headers, section titles, and important navigation accents.

Golden Amber - #E09F3E

Used for buttons, highlights, key labels, and call-to-action emphasis.

Warm Off-White - #FFF8EC

Used for page backgrounds and content card surfaces for readability.

5. Typography

  • Merriweather: Main headings (h1, h2) for a confident, formal chamber tone.
  • Source Sans 3: Body text, lists, and supporting descriptions for easy reading.
  • Fira Sans Condensed: Labels, section eyebrow text, and subtle UI accents.
