PAVI Projects: Final Project Site Plan

1. Site Name

Site Name: PAVI Projects

Optional domain: paviprojects.co.zw

The name was chosen to make the business identity clear while keeping the site focused on credibility, technology services, and professional communication for customers and partners.

2. Site Purpose

The website will introduce PAVI Projects to prospective clients, explain the products and services offered, and provide a clear path for visitors to request support, inquire about solutions, or contact the company. The goal is to build trust, present the business professionally, and make key information easy to find.

3. Audience and Scenarios

  • How can a customer learn about the products and services offered by PAVI Projects?
  • What technology solutions are available for homes, offices, and small businesses?
  • How can a visitor contact the company for a quote, question, or support request?

4. Color Scheme

The palette below creates a polished technology-brand feel with professional contrast and a calm, modern tone.

Deep Navy - #0D3B66

Used for headers, strong section titles, and high-priority navigation elements.

Electric Blue - #2B7FFF

Used for action buttons, links, and highlights to add energy and clarity.

Soft Cloud - #F8F8F2

Used for page backgrounds and card surfaces to keep the layout clean and easy to read.

5. Typography

  • Poppins: Main headings and key labels for a confident and modern businessvoice.
  • Inter: Body text, lists, and supporting descriptions for quick readability.
  • Source Sans 3: Supporting UI accents and secondary labels when needed.

6. Wireframe Direction

Two simple wireframes will guide the layout: a mobile-first home page with a clear hero section, business highlights, service cards, and a contact call-to-action, plus a desktop layout that presents the same information with more room for content.

Small View (Mobile)

A single-column layout with a hero banner, service summary, and contact prompt.

Large View (Desktop)

A wider layout with a split hero, featured offerings, and a stronger trust section.

Testing Checklist

Use these tools for self-evaluation before submission:

  • W3C Markup Validator for HTML validity.
  • WAVE for accessibility checks.
  • Google PageSpeed Insights or Lighthouse for performance and SEO.
  • WebAIM Contrast Checker for contrast compliance.
