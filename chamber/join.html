NP Membership

Free membership for verified non-profit organizations.

  • Basic directory listing
  • Access to public chamber events
  • Community announcements

Bronze Membership

Entry-level business membership for organizations growing their local reach.

  • Directory listing
  • Quarterly networking invites
  • Member pricing on selected events

Silver Membership

Growth membership with added visibility and training access.

  • Featured directory placement
  • Training workshop discounts
  • Occasional home-page spotlight consideration

Gold Membership

Premium membership with top-tier promotion and direct chamber support.

  • Priority homepage spotlight opportunities
  • Event sponsorship and advertising options
  • Priority invitations and stronger discount benefits
